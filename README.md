## Gabriel Alves Pedrosa

MSc student in Engineering Physics at the University of Minho, working on quantum
computing, from experimental quantum optics to quantum algorithms and variational
circuits.

### Projects

**[higgs-vqc](https://github.com/gabriel-a-pedrosa/higgs-vqc)**, a variational
quantum classifier for the ATLAS Higgs Boson Challenge. Trained on the five
most important features from a gradient boosting ranking, an average of five
circuits reaches a discovery significance of 2.93σ.
*PennyLane, scikit-learn, NumPy.*

**[quantum-grover-sat](https://github.com/gabriel-a-pedrosa/quantum-grover-sat)**,
Grover's algorithm for satisfiability problems, from Boolean formulas in CNF or
DNF to generalized *n* × *n* Sudoku, with a study of noise and zero-noise
extrapolation. *PennyLane.*

**[qobra-compiler](https://github.com/gabriel-a-pedrosa/qobra-compiler)**, the
compiler for Qobra, a programming language we designed, with a lexer, an LALR
parser and code generation for the EWVM stack machine. *Python, PLY.*

**[ieee1451-vehicle-monitor](https://github.com/gabriel-a-pedrosa/ieee1451-vehicle-monitor)**,
a smart-city prototype that detects vibrations on parked cars, with a PIC18
microcontroller acting as an IEEE 1451 smart transducer and a Python central
station that builds a safety map. *C, Assembly, Python.*

**[capacitive-keyboard](https://github.com/gabriel-a-pedrosa/capacitive-keyboard)**,
the KiCad design of a proximity-sensing keyboard where every key is a
capacitive sensor, read through analog multiplexers and a capacitance-to-digital
converter. *KiCad.*
